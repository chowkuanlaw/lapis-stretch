Lapis - stretch reminder
========================
Run Lapis.exe. Lapis sits in the system tray (bottom-right, near the clock;
it may be hidden under the ^ arrow - drag it onto the taskbar to keep it visible).

- Every 20 minutes a reminder card pops up in the bottom-right corner with one stretch.
  Start / Snooze / Skip, or "Different stretch".
- The small desktop widget shows the countdown. Drag it anywhere.
- Left-click the tray icon to open the Lapis window; right-click it for the menu (Pause, Quit).
- Closing the Lapis window keeps reminders running. Use "Quit Lapis" in the tray menu to stop.
- Lapis starts with Windows (turn off in Settings or the tray menu).
- Your stretch history is saved in %APPDATA%\Lapis\state.json.
